Full brief

Point a scan at an IDS, make it stealthy, then update the IDS to catch it. Description / Instructions Target: Point your scan at the assigned traffic-monitoring/IDS host (e.g., Snort, Suricata, or a packet-capture/monitoring VM). Setup & Run — Basic Scan: Run a basic network scan (for example, nmap -sS <target IP>) against the assigned lab target. Detect: Check the IDS/monitor logs and confirm whether the basic scan was detected. Save a log entry or screenshot. Stealth Mode: Modify your scan to make it stealthier (timing, packet flags, delays, fragmentation, -Pn, -sN, etc.) and run the stealth scan. Observe: Note whether the stealthy scan was detected. Save a log entry or screenshot. Hunt & Patch: Create or modify an IDS rule to detect the stealthy scan, apply/reload the rule, and document the rule you added/changed. Verify: Re-run the stealth scan and confirm the updated rule detects it. Save a log entry or screenshot. Submit: Initial detection log / screenshot Stealth-scan log / screenshot The IDS rule you added or modified (paste the rule text) Verification log / screenshot showing the stealth scan was detected after the rule change A 2–3 sentence summary explaining what you changed and why Notes & Rules Only perform scans inside the assigned lab environment. Do not scan external/public networks. Screenshots or log snippets are encouraged as evidence. Successful detection of the stealthy scan after updating the IDS is considered a strong outcome.
